# Contact glass-card styling

The four Let's Connect cards use an original HTML/CSS interpretation of the screenshot supplied by Omid: dark rounded frames, gray glass surfaces, layered circular highlights, monochrome icon badges, and subtle hover depth. Contact content, translations, mailto/tel links, and professional profile links are preserved.

Files: `index.html` and `contact-glass.css`. Styles are scoped under `#contact`; experience, education, projects, and booking retain their existing layouts. The grid adapts from four columns to two to one, with focus indicators and reduced-motion support.

Requested registry: https://21st.dev/r/Smit-Prajapati/glass-card

The registry returned Authentication required, and the component source tab displayed a paid-plan prompt. No registry source was downloaded or installed, and no access restriction was bypassed. This is an independent implementation from the user-supplied screenshot, not the original registry component. The static portfolio does not require React, shadcn, or npm for this treatment.
